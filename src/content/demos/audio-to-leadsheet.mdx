## How to read this demo

The first player is the original full-mix recording. The second is the melody line that
SheetSage-Pro extracted, re-synthesized so you can hear exactly what the model transcribed —
if the second player sounds like something you'd hum along to the first, the transcription is
doing its job.

Below the audio, the production version of this demo renders the full lead sheet: melody
notation, chord symbols above the staff, and section markers (intro / verse / chorus).

> **Note:** this is a static v1 demo with placeholder audio. Real examples from the evaluation
> set — with their rendered lead sheets and downloadable MusicXML — will replace these once the
> paper is out.

## What to listen for

- Does the melody follow the vocal line through melisma and syncopation?
- Are the chord symbols harmonically faithful, even when the voicing on the recording is sparse?
- Do repeated choruses map to the same section, rather than three slightly different ones?
