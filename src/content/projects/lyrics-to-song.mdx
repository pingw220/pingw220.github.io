## Overview

This project at the [Music and AI Lab](https://affige.github.io/lab.html) at National Taiwan
University, advised by Prof. [Yi-Hsuan Yang](https://affige.github.io/), builds an end-to-end
pipeline that turns lyrics into a song. A symbolic **lead sheet** comes first, then it is passed
to downstream arrangement and audio synthesis.

## Approach

Language models are fine-tuned to generate melody, chords, and rhythm together with a
**lyric-aligned symbolic representation**, including:

- syllable-level lyric pointers and phonetic information,
- rests and breaths,
- many-to-many lyric–note alignment, so one syllable can span several notes and vice versa.

Representing the lyrics explicitly makes the generated song controllable at the level of
individual words and syllables.
