## Overview

Work at the [Ubiquitous Computing Lab](https://ubicomplab.cs.washington.edu/) at the University
of Washington: designing and optimizing lightweight neural networks that recognize gestures in
real time on resource-constrained embedded devices. This included model quantization and
deployment for on-device inference.
