## Overview

Work at the [Ubiquitous Computing Lab](https://ubicomplab.cs.washington.edu/) at the University
of Washington on screening for disease from cough sounds.

- **Cough detection and localization:** models that find cough events and mark their timing
  in continuous audio recordings.
- **Tuberculosis screening:** transformer-based architectures, including the Audio
  Spectrogram Transformer (AST) and MobileViT, applied to cough-based tuberculosis risk
  classification.
